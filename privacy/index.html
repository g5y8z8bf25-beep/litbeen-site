Litbeen 1.0

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Litbeen is a local-first personal travel application. This policy describes what the Litbeen app processes, what remains on your device, when third-party services receive a request, and how the official Litbeen website operates.

In short

  • No Litbeen account is required.
  • Litbeen does not operate a server that receives your travel history.
  • No advertising or analytics SDK is included.
  • Optional Wikipedia information is fetched only when you request it.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Litbeen 1.0 for iPhone and iPad and to this official website. Apple, GitHub, Wikimedia, and other third parties have their own privacy practices for services they operate.

2. Information processed by the app

When you mark or remove a visited city, Litbeen creates a local journey record. A record can contain the GeoNames identifier; city, region, country or territory names and codes; latitude and longitude; time zone; catalogue version; first-visited, updated, and snapshot timestamps; a locally generated revision identifier; and a state showing whether the place is visited or removed.

Search text, nearby-city results, globe position, and optional Wikipedia content are used to provide the current experience. Litbeen does not add them to your journey history unless you explicitly mark a city as visited. Wikipedia content is held only in an in-memory cache for the current app session.

3. Local storage and retention

Journey records are stored in the app's local web storage on your device. Litbeen 1.0 does not automatically synchronize them to a Litbeen account or cloud database. The app also preserves its earlier local-storage record during migration so it does not silently destroy older history.

An active record remains until you remove the visited status or the app's local data is deleted. Removing a visit changes the record to a deletion marker, called a tombstone. Tombstones are retained locally so importing an older backup cannot silently restore a city you removed. Litbeen 1.0 does not provide a separate control for purging individual tombstones.

Deleting the app or clearing its website/app data may delete locally stored history. Litbeen therefore provides manual JSON backup tools. Device-level backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by Litbeen.

4. JSON backup and restore

Export creates a JSON file on your device containing active visits and tombstones, including the journey fields described above. Copy Backup JSON places the same data on your device clipboard. Litbeen does not upload either form to a Litbeen server. Where you save, paste, or share a backup is your choice and may involve another app or service with its own privacy practices.

Import reads a backup you select, validates it locally, and deterministically merges newer records into local history. Invalid or unsupported files are rejected rather than replacing the existing journey.

5. Network requests and third parties

Optional place information

When you choose Explore this place, Litbeen sends direct HTTPS requests from your device to Wikidata and English Wikipedia. Request parameters can include a GeoNames identifier, city name, Wikidata item identifier, or Wikipedia article title. If a freely licensed image is available, the image can be loaded from Wikimedia infrastructure. These services necessarily receive standard connection information such as your IP address and browser or device request headers.

See the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy for Wikimedia's practices. Litbeen does not send your complete visited-city list to Wikimedia.

Bundled geographic and visual resources

The GeoNames city catalogue, NASA Blue Marble Earth texture, and core application resources are packaged with the iOS app. Using search or viewing the Earth does not contact GeoNames or NASA. Natural Earth review resources are not loaded by the active Litbeen 1.0 globe.

Apple distribution

Apple processes App Store and TestFlight downloads, purchases, diagnostics, and related account activity under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Litbeen does not receive your Apple ID password or payment information.

6. Permissions, analytics, and advertising

Litbeen 1.0 does not request access to your location, photos, camera, microphone, or contacts. It does not scan your photo library. The app contains no Litbeen account system, advertising SDK, cross-app tracking SDK, or third-party analytics SDK.

Development-only performance diagnostics are not displayed in the normal production experience and do not send their readings to Litbeen.

7. Official website

This website is a static GitHub Pages site. Litbeen does not add analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, cookies, or local storage to it. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and logs, to host and secure GitHub Pages. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Security and your choices

Local-first storage limits routine data transmission, but no storage method is free of risk. Protect access to your device and store exported backups securely. You can stop optional Wikimedia requests by not opening Explore this place, and you can use the core globe, local catalogue, and existing journey without that feature.

9. Children

Litbeen is a general-audience travel utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Litbeen account or backend because neither is present in version 1.0.

10. Support communications

This website has no contact form. If you choose to email Litbeen Support, your email provider sends the address, message, and any attachments you include to the mailbox serving the address below. That information is used to respond to and resolve your request. Litbeen 1.0 has no automated support-message retention schedule. Please do not send a journey backup unless support specifically asks and you choose to share it.

11. Changes to this policy

If Litbeen's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. A future account, cloud synchronization, or photo feature would require a policy update before release.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact litbeen@tutamail.com.
