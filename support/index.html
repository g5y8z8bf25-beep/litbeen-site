Backup & restore

Keep a copy you control.

Litbeen does not use an account or cloud sync. A JSON backup is the safest way to move or preserve your journey before changing devices or deleting the app.

Export a backup

  1. Open My Places.
  2. Expand Backup & restore.
  3. Choose Export JSON and save the file in a location you trust.

You can also choose Copy Backup JSON when a file download is unavailable.

Restore a backup

  1. Open My Places and expand Backup & restore.
  2. Choose Import JSON.
  3. Select an unmodified Litbeen journey file and review the result.

Import merges newer records. It does not blindly replace your current journey.

Frequently asked questions

Useful answers.

Where is my travel history stored?

On your device in Litbeen's local app storage. Version 1.0 has no Litbeen account or cloud database.

Does marking one city mark the whole country?

No. Only cities you explicitly mark are recorded and shown as permanent golden beacons.

Can I use Litbeen offline?

The bundled Earth, city catalogue, and saved journey are available without Litbeen's development server. Optional Wikipedia place information requires a network connection.

Why does a removed city remain in a backup?

Litbeen retains a deletion marker so an older backup cannot silently restore a city you intentionally removed.

How do I move my journey to another device?

Export a JSON backup on the original device, transfer it securely, then import it from My Places on the new device.

What should I try if the Earth does not appear?

Close and reopen Litbeen, confirm the device is running a supported iOS version, and try again. Your local travel history is not cleared by a normal restart.

Still need help?

Contact Litbeen Support

Email litbeen@tutamail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, Litbeen version, and a brief description of what happened. Do not attach your journey backup unless support specifically asks and you choose to share it.
